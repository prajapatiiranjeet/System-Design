Sharding

Step 1 / 9
Problem

Too big for one machine

The users database has grown to 20 TB and takes 40,000 writes a second. Followers would help with reads — but every write still has to go through one leader, and all the data has to fit on its disks.

You’re already on the biggest machine, and every backup, restore or new replica now takes the best part of a day to copy.

Watch out

Replication copies all the data everywhere. To scale writes and storage, the data itself has to be split.
